How do I play?

Swipe to change which way gravity pulls, and tap a group of 3 or more matching gems to pop it. Popping 4 or more makes a numbered bomb; match a bomb into a group of its color to set it off. The full guide is under How to play on the main menu.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone, inside the app. Deleting the app deletes your progress, Stardust and stats, and they can't be recovered.

How do I start over?

Open Settings in the game and use the reset option. This clears your missions, scores and Stardust on this device.

How do I turn off music, sound or vibration?

In Settings, or from the pause menu during a mission.

What stays on your device

To work, the game saves a few things in its own storage on your iPhone:

  • Your mission progress, best scores, medals and stats
  • Your Stardust, boosters and the themes and music you've unlocked
  • Your settings, such as sound, music and vibration

This information is never sent to me or to anyone else. It is deleted when you delete the app, or when you reset your progress in Settings.

Internet and third parties

The game plays fully offline and does not connect to any server. It contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Children

Gravity Gems is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds something that uses data, such as an online leaderboard or in-app purchases, this policy will be updated first and the date above will change. The App Store's privacy details will be updated at the same time.
